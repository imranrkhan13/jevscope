How to read this

Two independent properties are measured. Calibration (ECE, reliability diagram) asks whether a stated 0.9 really means 90%. Discrimination (AURC, risk-coverage) asks whether the confidence ordering separates right answers from wrong ones. A model can have either without the other, and routing needs both: discrimination decides whether a threshold helps at all, calibration decides where to put it without re-measuring.

Cost model

One automated error costs 50; one human review costs 1; reviewers are assumed 100% accurate. Only the ratio matters. Every threshold above is a consequence of these three numbers — change them and re-run rather than arguing about the default.
